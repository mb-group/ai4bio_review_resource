Five things to remember

  1. Foundation models give versatility; agentic systems give accessibility. Integrating them needs a shared language about reliability.
  2. That language is the knowledge boundary: the inputs, tasks and conditions where a model's output can be trusted.
  3. Five design elements (data, architecture, training scheme, evaluation scheme, post-analysis) set that boundary for any model.
  4. Evidence shows real learned biology, but also simple baselines that often match the models. Benchmarks, confidence and application reliability must be established separately.
  5. Release every model with a structured boundary statement, and keep a community registry, so researchers and agents know when to call it.
